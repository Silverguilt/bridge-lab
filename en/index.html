Understanding the simulation

The test checks for two side structures, longitudinal members, cross ties and diagonals. Deflection is an illustrative geometry-based score; stability is estimated separately from geometry.

Craft sticks use real base dimensions. This version does not calculate glued joint strength. The physical bridge requires supervised load testing.

Future physics development

• Individual glue joints and overlaps
• Member tension, compression and buckling
• 3D finite elements and actual load paths
• Deck bending and lateral stability
• Calibration using physical samples
